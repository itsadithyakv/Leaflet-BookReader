> Pip, a tiny companion who cheers you on.

## Description

> Leaflet is a book reader for the books you already own. Drop in your EPUBs,
> PDFs and comics and they stay on your computer: no store, no subscription,
> nothing locked.
>
> **A library that sorts itself**
> Own a whole series? Leaflet puts it together in reading order, even when the
> files don't say, and tells you what's next when you finish a book, or which
> book you're missing. Shelves for what you're reading, what you've finished,
> what you've paused and what you just added keep themselves up to date. Make
> your own collections too.
>
> **Reading the way you like it**
> Scroll or turn pages. Search the whole book. Highlight in four colours, add
> notes, and copy them all out as Markdown. Pick your text size, a comfortable
> line length, and a page from warm paper to true black. Smart Read's guide dot
> learns how fast you read, book by book, and waits while you stop to think; or
> try SpeedRead's one word at a time, or let auto-scroll carry you.
>
> **A habit, not a chore**
> Set a daily goal and build a streak, with freezes and a grace day for busy
> weeks. Start a focus session and watch your finished sessions line up on your
> bookshelf. Read in full screen and grow a focus flower: it blooms if you stay
> to the end. Gentle reminders arrive as Windows notifications.
>
> **Meet Pip**
> Pip lives in the app, perched on the Leaflet logo. It celebrates your
> finished books, naps at night, and grows a garden from the time you spend
> reading. Its house is a little game of its own: pick what your reading grew,
> fill the rooms, dress Pip up, and grant its wish of the day.
>
> **Yours, privately**
> Back up your books to your own Google Drive; Leaflet can see only the files
> it made there. An account is optional: signed in, your places, highlights
> and reading days are kept in step across your devices with it. So is the
> community: weekly leaderboards, kudos and friendly duels with other
> readers, with a profile that stays private until you choose otherwise.
>
> Opens EPUB, PDF, CBZ, TXT, HTML and FB2 out of the box, and 19 more formats
> (MOBI, AZW3, DOCX and others) with the free Calibre app installed.
