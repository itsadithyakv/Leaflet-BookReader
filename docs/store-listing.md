> A free, private book reader for your own EPUBs, PDFs and comics, with series
> that organise themselves, highlights and notes, a daily reading streak, and
> Pip, a tiny companion who cheers you on.

## Description

> Leaflet is a book reader for the books you already own. Drop in your EPUBs,
> PDFs and comics and they stay on your computer: no store, no subscription,
> nothing locked.
>
> **A library that sorts itself**
> Own a whole series? Leaflet puts it together in reading order, even when the
> files don't say, and tells you what's next when you finish a book, or which
> book you're missing. Shelves for what you're reading, what you've finished,
> what you've paused and what you just added keep themselves up to date. Make
> your own collections too.
>
> **Reading the way you like it**
> Scroll or turn pages. Search the whole book. Highlight in four colours, add
> notes, and copy them all out as Markdown. Pick your text size, a comfortable
> line length, and a page from warm paper to true black. Smart Read's guide dot
> learns how fast you read, book by book, and waits while you stop to think; or
> try SpeedRead's one word at a time, or let auto-scroll carry you.
>
> **A habit, not a chore**
> Set a daily goal and build a streak, with freezes and a grace day for busy
> weeks. Start a focus session and watch your finished sessions line up on your
> bookshelf. Read in full screen and grow a focus flower: it blooms if you stay
> to the end. Gentle reminders arrive as Windows notifications.
>
> **Meet Pip**
> Pip lives in the app, perched on the Leaflet logo. It celebrates your
> finished books, naps at night, and grows a garden from the time you spend
> reading. Its house is a little game of its own: pick what your reading grew,
> fill the rooms, dress Pip up, and grant its wish of the day.
>
> **Yours, privately**
> Back up your library, progress and highlights to your own Google Drive.
> Leaflet can see only the files it made there. An account is optional, and so
> is the community: weekly leaderboards, kudos and friendly duels with other
> readers, with a profile that stays private until you choose otherwise.
>
> Opens EPUB, PDF, CBZ, TXT, HTML and FB2 out of the box, and 19 more formats
> (MOBI, AZW3, DOCX and others) with the free Calibre app installed.

## What's new in this version

For 1.4.0 (the first release after 1.3.2; the Store's field takes 1,500
characters, this is about 1,450):

> Friends and a place to talk about books, all optional. Follow readers, see
> what they are reading, and open the library of anyone who shares theirs.
> Post a finished book, a proper review or a quote from your highlights, to
> your followers or to everyone, and like or dislike what others post.
> Discover is a feed of reviews and quotes from readers everywhere. Every
> book and series can have its own community with threads for theories;
> spoilers stay covered until you have read that far. Report and block are
> on everything.
>
> Book clubs with a shared weekly goal, badges for hard milestones, levels,
> duels with seeds staked on them, and presents for a friend's Pip.
>
> More to do with seeds: a streak freeze, a stake on your own reading goal,
> rarer things in Pip's shop that open at higher levels, and three new
> sounds for the reading room.
>
> Your library: PDFs get their cover without being opened, genres are a wall
> of tiles in Collections, a series can change which set of covers it wears,
> The Hobbit sits with The Lord of the Rings, and a file over 10 MB asks
> before it is backed up. Every session on your shelf is named after the book
> you were reading.
>
> New in Pip's arcade: the Hollow, a camp your reading builds. Lay it out,
> hold it against the magpies at night, raid their camps with Pip's scouts,
> and bring along heroes who turn up as you finish books. They live in the
> camp, and Pip goes for walks while you read and comes back with tales.
>
> Pip's arcade now fills the window, and each game plays on its own card
> before you pick it. A game that ends stays on screen so you can see how it
> went.
> You can remove a book from any card's menu.

2. Series gather by themselves, in reading order, and tell you what's next

17. Works offline; your books never leave your computer
18. Free, with nothing locked
19. Full-screen focus sessions that grow a flower as you read
20. A comfortable line length on wide screens, or the full width if you prefer
