# Leaflet Community Guidelines

**Who we are:** Leaflet is made by PaperKite. Contact: [adithyakrishnan.vinod@gmail.com](mailto:adithyakrishnan.vinod@gmail.com)

Leaflet is a reading app. Most of it is yours alone, on your own device. The
community is the part that is not: the posts you choose to show other
readers, and the communities where readers talk a book over. All of it is
optional. Nothing is posted unless you post it, a post goes only to the
readers who follow you unless you choose "Everyone" for it, and you are in no
community you did not join.

These are the rules for what is posted where other readers can see it. The
app shows them, in short, the first time you post for everyone or join a
community, and asks you to agree.

## The rules

1. **Talk about books.** Reviews, quotes, questions, theories, favourite
   parts. Not adverts, not things for sale, not links to downloads of books,
   and not anything else that is here for a reason other than reading.
2. **Be kind to the reader, hard on the book.** Disagree as much as you like
   with what someone thinks of a book or an author's work. No insults,
   threats or harassment, and no hate towards anyone for who they are.
3. **Mark your spoilers.** A thread says how far into its book it goes, and
   a review or a quote that gives the book away says so. Leaflet keeps what
   is past a reader's own place covered until they ask to see it.
4. **Quote a little, not a lot.** A few lines to show what you mean. Whole
   pages and chapters belong to the author. A quote can be up to 500
   characters, and a reader can post up to 30 from one book.
5. **Nothing unlawful, and nothing sexual or violent for its own sake.**
   Nothing that puts a child at risk, ever. Such posts are removed and the
   account is suspended.
6. **One account, yours.** No pretending to be someone else, and no second
   accounts to get round a suspension, pad a community or pile on reports.

## Communities

There is one community for each book and one for each series, named after
the book: nobody chooses a community's name, so there is nothing to squat on
or to name offensively. The first reader to open a book's community starts
it and looks after it (its "keeper"): they can pin threads, and remove
threads, replies and members that break these rules in it. A community shows
in the directory once three readers have joined it. To start one you need a
public profile, a confirmed email address and an account at least a week
old, and you can start one a day.

## What happens when something breaks the rules

- **Anyone can report** a post, a thread, a reply, a community or a reader,
  from the thing itself. Reports go to us, not to the person reported, and
  we do not say who reported.
- **What several readers report is hidden straight away**, before anyone has
  looked: three reports hide a post, a thread or a reply; a thread reported
  twice for an unmarked spoiler is marked as one. We then look, and either
  put it back or remove it.
- **We can remove** a post, a thread, a reply or a whole community, and
  **suspend an account** from posting, for days or for good.
- **You can block any reader.** You stop seeing each other's posts, threads
  and replies, and neither can follow, challenge or send the other anything.
  They are not told. You can unblock them under Social → Board → You.
- If something is urgent or puts someone in danger, write to us at the
  address above as well as reporting it.

If you think we got it wrong (something of yours was removed, or left up),
write to the same address and we will look again.

## What we can and cannot see

To review a report we see the thing reported, as it was posted, and who
posted it. We do not see your library, your notes or your highlights, which
are not on our server unless you shared or backed them up (see the
[privacy policy](privacy-policy.md)). Posts for your followers only are seen
by us only if one of those followers reports them.

## Changes

If these rules change we will update this page and, for a change that
matters, say so in the app.
