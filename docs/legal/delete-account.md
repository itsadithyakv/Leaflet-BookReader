# Delete your Leaflet account

You can delete your Leaflet account, and everything our server holds about
you, at any time. You do not need the app installed to ask.

## In the app (straight away)

1. Open Leaflet on any computer or phone where you are signed in.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**.
3. Type your password and press **Delete for good**.

Forgotten your password? Press **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen,
reset it, and then delete the account as above.

## Without the app

Email [adithyakrishnan.vinod@gmail.com](mailto:adithyakrishnan.vinod@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Leaflet%20account)
from the address your account uses, with the subject **Delete my Leaflet
account**. So that nobody can delete someone else's account, we reply to
that address to confirm before we delete it. We delete it within 7 days of
your confirmation and tell you when it is done.

## What is deleted

Straight away: your account (email address and hashed password), your
profile, your synced reading state (places, reading days, highlights, notes,
bookmarks, collections and Pip), every sign-in session, your shared library,
your posts, your club memberships, and every follow, request, kudos, duel,
present, notification and problem report that involves you.

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Encrypted backups** of our server's database are kept for 14 days, and
  never more than 30. Your data leaves them as they expire. If we ever have to
  restore one, we delete your account from it again.
- Where you appear in another reader's kudos lists, an internal number that
  no longer leads to anyone stays, so the badge they earned stays theirs.
- If your account was suspended from posting, a keyed hash of its email
  address (not the address) is kept until the suspension ends, so that it is
  not escaped by making a new account.

## What is not ours to delete

Your book files and your settings never reach our server; they stay on your
devices. If you backed up to your own Google Drive, the "Leaflet" folder
there is yours: delete it from Drive if you want it gone. Uninstalling the
app removes what it kept on that device.

The full details are in our [privacy policy](../privacy/).
